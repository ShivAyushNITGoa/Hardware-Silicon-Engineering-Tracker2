# Semiconductor Research Paper Roadmap

## Beginner Level

Study:
- Semiconductor device basics
- CMOS scaling
- Digital design fundamentals

Recommended topics:
- MOSFET operation
- CMOS inverter
- Technology scaling

## Intermediate Level

Topics:
- FinFET
- GAAFET
- Low power VLSI
- Advanced packaging

## Advanced Level

Topics:
- 3D IC
- Chiplets
- AI accelerators
- HBM
- Quantum devices

## Reading Method

1. Understand abstract
2. Study architecture
3. Analyze equations
4. Reproduce experiments
5. Compare with current technology

Sources:
- IEEE
- ACM
- Nature Electronics
- VLSI Symposium
