# Carrier Mobility

## 1. Overview
Carrier mobility describes how easily electrons and holes move through a semiconductor under an electric field.

## 2. Prerequisites
- Energy bands
- Electrons and holes
- Electric fields
- Crystal structure

## 3. Concept
Mobility depends on:
- Crystal quality
- Temperature
- Doping concentration
- Scattering mechanisms

## 4. Equation
Drift velocity:

v_d = μE

where:
- v_d = drift velocity
- μ = mobility
- E = electric field

Current density:

J = q n μ E

## 5. Important Effects
- Phonon scattering
- Impurity scattering
- Surface scattering in MOSFETs

## 6. Industry Importance
Higher mobility enables:
- Faster transistors
- Lower power operation
- Better RF performance

## 7. Applications
- CMOS processors
- Power devices
- RF chips
- Sensors
