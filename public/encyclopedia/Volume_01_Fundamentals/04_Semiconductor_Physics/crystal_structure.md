# Crystal Structure of Semiconductors

## Prerequisites
- Atomic structure
- Chemical bonding
- Solid state physics

## Overview
Semiconductor materials are arranged in periodic crystal structures. The arrangement of atoms determines electrical, optical and mechanical properties.

## Important Concepts

### Crystal Lattice
A repeating three-dimensional arrangement of atoms.

### Unit Cell
The smallest repeating structure representing a crystal.

## Silicon Crystal Structure

Silicon has a diamond cubic structure.

Characteristics:
- Four valence electrons
- Covalent bonding
- Stable CMOS material

## Other Semiconductor Structures

| Material | Structure | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Silicon | Diamond cubic | CMOS ICs |
| GaAs | Zinc blende | RF/optoelectronics |
| GaN | Wurtzite | Power electronics |
| SiC | Hexagonal | High voltage devices |

## Importance in Semiconductor Industry
Crystal structure affects:
- Carrier mobility
- Band gap
- Defects
- Manufacturing process
- Device performance
