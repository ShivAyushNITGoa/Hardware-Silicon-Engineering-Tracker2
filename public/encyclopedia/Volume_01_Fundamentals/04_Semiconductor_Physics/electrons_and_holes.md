# Electrons and Holes in Semiconductors

## Prerequisites
- Atomic structure
- Energy bands
- Quantum physics basics

## Overview
Electrical conduction in semiconductors occurs through two charge carriers:

1. Electrons
2. Holes

## Electrons

Electrons are negatively charged particles that move in the conduction band.

Properties:
- Charge: -1.6 × 10^-19 C
- Responsible for electron current

## Holes

A hole represents the absence of an electron in the valence band.

Properties:
- Behaves as positive charge carrier
- Responsible for hole current

## Carrier Movement

Electrons and holes move due to:

- Electric fields (drift)
- Concentration gradients (diffusion)

## Importance
Carrier behavior determines:

- Diode operation
- MOSFET performance
- Leakage current
- Semiconductor speed
