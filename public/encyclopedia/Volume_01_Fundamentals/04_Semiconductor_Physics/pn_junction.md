# PN Junction

## 1. Overview
A PN junction is formed by joining P-type and N-type semiconductor regions. It is the foundation of diodes, transistors, and many IC devices.

## 2. Prerequisites
- Doping
- Carrier concentration
- Energy bands
- Fermi level

## 3. Formation
When P and N materials join:
- Electrons diffuse from N to P
- Holes diffuse from P to N
- Depletion region forms
- Built-in electric field develops

## 4. Bias Conditions

### Forward Bias
- Reduces depletion width
- Allows current flow

### Reverse Bias
- Increases depletion width
- Small leakage current

## 5. Important Concepts
- Depletion region
- Built-in potential
- Junction capacitance
- Breakdown voltage

## 6. Applications
- Diodes
- LEDs
- Solar cells
- BJT transistors
- Protection circuits
