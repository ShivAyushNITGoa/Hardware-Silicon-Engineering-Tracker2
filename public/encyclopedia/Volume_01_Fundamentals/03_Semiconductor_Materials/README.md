# Semiconductor Materials

## Major Materials

## Silicon (Si)

- Most widely used semiconductor
- CMOS technology foundation

## Germanium (Ge)

- Lower band gap
- Historical semiconductor material

## Gallium Nitride (GaN)

Applications:
- Power electronics
- RF devices
- Fast switching

## Silicon Carbide (SiC)

Applications:
- Electric vehicles
- High voltage devices
- Power converters

## Comparison Parameters

- Band gap
- Mobility
- Thermal conductivity
- Breakdown voltage
- Manufacturing maturity
