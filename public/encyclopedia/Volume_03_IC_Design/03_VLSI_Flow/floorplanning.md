# Floorplanning in VLSI

## Overview
Floorplanning is the first physical design stage where major blocks, macros, IOs, and power structures are arranged on the chip.

## Objectives
- Minimize area
- Reduce wire length
- Improve timing
- Enable power distribution

## Steps
1. Die size estimation
2. Core area definition
3. Macro placement
4. IO planning
5. Power planning

## Important Concepts
- Utilization
- Aspect ratio
- Placement density
- Congestion
- Routing resources
