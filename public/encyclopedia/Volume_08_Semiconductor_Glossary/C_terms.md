# C Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## CAD — Computer Aided Design

**Definition:**
Computer-based tools used for engineering design and analysis.

**Semiconductor usage:**
- IC design
- Layout
- Simulation
- Verification

---

## CMOS — Complementary Metal Oxide Semiconductor

**Definition:**
A technology using complementary NMOS and PMOS transistors.

**Advantages:**
- Low power consumption
- High noise immunity
- High density

**Applications:**
- CPUs
- GPUs
- SoCs
- Memory

---

## CMP — Chemical Mechanical Planarization

**Definition:**
A semiconductor manufacturing process used to flatten wafer surfaces.

**Importance:**
Enables multilayer interconnect fabrication.

---

## CDR — Clock Data Recovery

**Definition:**
A circuit that extracts timing information from high-speed data streams.

**Applications:**
- High-speed communication
- SerDes
- Optical links

---

## CDC — Clock Domain Crossing

**Definition:**
Transfer of signals between different clock domains.

**Challenges:**
- Metastability
- Data loss
- Synchronization

---

## Chiplet

**Definition:**
A small functional silicon die combined with other dies to create a larger system.

**Advantages:**
- Better yield
- Reusable IP
- Advanced integration

---

## Critical Dimension (CD)

**Definition:**
The smallest printable feature size in semiconductor manufacturing.

**Importance:**
Controls transistor scaling and process capability.

---

## Clock Tree Synthesis (CTS)

**Definition:**
ASIC physical design process that creates balanced clock distribution networks.

**Goals:**
- Reduce clock skew
- Improve timing

---

## Contamination Control

**Definition:**
Methods used in fabs to prevent particles and impurities during fabrication.

**Importance:**
Improves yield and reliability.
