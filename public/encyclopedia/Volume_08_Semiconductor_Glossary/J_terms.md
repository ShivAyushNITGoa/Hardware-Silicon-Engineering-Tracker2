# J-Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## JTAG — Joint Test Action Group
- IEEE standard for boundary scan testing.
- Used for IC testing, debugging and programming.

## Junction
- Region where two semiconductor materials meet.
- Examples: PN junction, transistor junctions.

## Junction Depth
- Depth of doped region below silicon surface.
- Important parameter in fabrication.

## Joule Heating
- Heat generated due to electrical resistance.
- Considered in reliability and thermal analysis.
