# Semiconductor Reliability Dictionary

## Overview
Semiconductor reliability studies how ICs maintain performance over lifetime under electrical, thermal and environmental stress.

## Electromigration
Metal atom movement caused by high current density.

Effects:
- Open circuits
- Voids
- Interconnect failure

## TDDB — Time Dependent Dielectric Breakdown
Gate oxide degradation due to electrical stress.

## BTI — Bias Temperature Instability
Threshold voltage shift caused by voltage and temperature stress.

## HCI — Hot Carrier Injection
Carrier energy causing device degradation.

## Failure Mechanisms
- Electromigration
- Thermal cycling
- Package cracking
- Dielectric breakdown
- Aging effects

## Reliability Analysis Tools
- TCAD
- SPICE reliability models
- Ansys analysis tools
- EDA signoff tools
