# K Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## KLA
**KLA Corporation**

- Semiconductor process control and inspection equipment company.
- Provides defect inspection and metrology systems.
- Important for yield improvement in fabs.

## Kelvin Test

- Four-wire measurement technique.
- Used for accurate resistance measurement in semiconductor testing.

## Kerf

- Area between dies on a semiconductor wafer.
- Contains scribe lines used for wafer cutting.

## KGD — Known Good Die

- Tested semiconductor die before packaging.
- Important for advanced multi-chip packages.

## K-Map — Karnaugh Map

- Boolean logic simplification method.
- Used in digital circuit design.
