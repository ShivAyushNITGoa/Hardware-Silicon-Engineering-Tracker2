# Q-Terms Semiconductor Glossary

## QFN — Quad Flat No-lead Package

- Surface mount semiconductor package.
- Provides compact size and good thermal performance.
- Used in mobile, IoT and embedded devices.

## QFP — Quad Flat Package

- Leaded IC package technology.
- Common in microcontrollers and older ASIC packages.

## Quantization

- Conversion of continuous analog values into discrete digital levels.
- Important in ADC design and signal processing.

## Quantum Well

- Semiconductor structure where carriers are confined in thin layers.
- Used in lasers, LEDs and advanced devices.

## QOS — Quality of Service

- Performance management concept in networking SoCs.
- Controls bandwidth and priority handling.
