# F Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## FEOL — Front End Of Line
The transistor fabrication stage of semiconductor manufacturing.

Covers:
- Well formation
- Gate formation
- Source/drain implantation
- Transistor creation

## FinFET
A 3D transistor architecture using a fin-shaped channel.

Advantages:
- Better electrostatic control
- Lower leakage
- Higher performance

## FPGA — Field Programmable Gate Array
A reconfigurable semiconductor device containing LUTs, flip-flops and programmable routing.

Applications:
- Prototyping
- Acceleration
- Embedded systems

## Flip Chip
Packaging method where the die is connected directly using solder bumps.

Benefits:
- High speed
- Better thermal performance

## Floorplanning
ASIC physical design step where major blocks, macros and IO locations are planned.

## Functional Verification
Process of checking whether a design meets its functional specification.

Methods:
- Simulation
- Assertions
- Coverage
