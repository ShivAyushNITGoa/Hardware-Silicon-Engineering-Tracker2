# T Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## Tapeout
Final release of semiconductor design data to manufacturing.

Applications:
- ASIC production
- Foundry manufacturing

## TCAD — Technology Computer-Aided Design
Simulation tools for semiconductor process and device analysis.

Used for:
- MOSFET modeling
- Process optimization
- Device research

## TSV — Through Silicon Via
Vertical electrical connection through silicon dies.

Applications:
- 3D IC
- HBM memory
- Chip stacking

## Timing Closure
Process of meeting all timing requirements in a digital design.

Includes:
- Setup timing
- Hold timing
- Clock optimization

## Transistor
Fundamental semiconductor switching device.

Types:
- MOSFET
- BJT
- FinFET
- GAAFET
