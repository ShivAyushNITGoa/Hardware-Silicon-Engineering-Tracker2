# I-Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## IC — Integrated Circuit
- Complete electronic circuit fabricated on semiconductor substrate.
- Contains transistors, resistors, capacitors and interconnects.

## Ion Implantation
- Process of introducing dopant ions into silicon.
- Used for source/drain formation and threshold adjustment.

## IR Drop
- Voltage drop caused by resistance in power delivery network.
- Important in power integrity analysis.

## Interconnect
- Metal wiring connecting devices inside an IC.
- Major challenge in advanced nodes.

## Inverter
- Fundamental CMOS logic gate.
- Used as building block for digital circuits.
