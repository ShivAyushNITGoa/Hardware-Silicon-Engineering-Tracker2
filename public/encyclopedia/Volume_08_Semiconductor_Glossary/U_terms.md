# U Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## UVM — Universal Verification Methodology
Industry standard SystemVerilog verification methodology.

Concepts:
- Testbench architecture
- Agents
- Drivers
- Monitors
- Coverage

## Underfill
Material used in advanced packaging to improve mechanical reliability.

Applications:
- Flip chip packages
- High-performance ICs

## Ultra Low Power Design
Design methodology focused on minimizing energy consumption.

Techniques:
- Power gating
- Clock gating
- DVFS

## UART — Universal Asynchronous Receiver Transmitter
Serial communication interface used in embedded systems.

Applications:
- MCU communication
- Debug interfaces
