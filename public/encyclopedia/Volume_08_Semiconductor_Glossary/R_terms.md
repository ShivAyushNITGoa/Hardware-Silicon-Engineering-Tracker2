# R-Terms Semiconductor Glossary

## RTL — Register Transfer Level

- Hardware description abstraction level.
- Used for digital IC design using Verilog/SystemVerilog.

## RISC-V

- Open instruction set architecture.
- Used for CPUs, SoCs and research processors.

## Routing

- Physical design step connecting placed cells.
- Includes global and detailed routing.

## Resist

- Light-sensitive material used in photolithography.
- Enables wafer pattern transfer.

## Reliability Engineering

- Ensures semiconductor devices operate correctly over lifetime.
- Includes electromigration and aging analysis.
