# CMOS Comparison Tables

## CMOS vs NMOS

| Feature | CMOS | NMOS |
|---|---|---|
| Power | Very low static power | Higher power |
| Noise immunity | High | Lower |
| Modern usage | Dominant | Historical |

## SRAM vs DRAM

| Feature | SRAM | DRAM |
|---|---|---|
| Cell | 6T | 1T + capacitor |
| Speed | Faster | Slower |
| Density | Lower | Higher |

## Planar CMOS vs FinFET

| Feature | Planar | FinFET |
|---|---|---|
| Gate control | Single side | Three sides |
| Leakage | Higher | Lower |
| Scaling | Limited | Better |
