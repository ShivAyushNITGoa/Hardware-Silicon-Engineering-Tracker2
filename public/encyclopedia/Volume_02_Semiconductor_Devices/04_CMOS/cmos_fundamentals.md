# CMOS Fundamentals

## Overview
CMOS (Complementary Metal Oxide Semiconductor) is the dominant technology used for modern digital integrated circuits.

Applications:
- CPUs
- GPUs
- SoCs
- Microcontrollers
- Memory chips
- AI accelerators

## Basic Principle
CMOS combines:

- NMOS transistor (pull-down network)
- PMOS transistor (pull-up network)

The complementary operation provides:

- Low static power consumption
- High noise immunity
- High density

## CMOS Evolution

```text
PMOS Logic
   ↓
NMOS Logic
   ↓
CMOS
   ↓
FinFET CMOS
   ↓
GAAFET CMOS
```
