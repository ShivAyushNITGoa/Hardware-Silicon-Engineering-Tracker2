# CMOS Transmission Gate

## Introduction

Transmission gates are bidirectional CMOS switches made using:
- NMOS transistor
- PMOS transistor

## Working

NMOS passes strong 0 and weak 1.
PMOS passes strong 1 and weak 0.
Together they pass both logic levels efficiently.

## Applications

- Multiplexers
- Latches
- Flip-flops
- Analog switches
- Sample and hold circuits

## Advantages

- Low resistance
- Full voltage swing
- Bidirectional operation
