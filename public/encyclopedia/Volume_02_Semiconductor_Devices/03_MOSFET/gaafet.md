# GAAFET (Gate-All-Around FET)

## Overview

GAAFET is an advanced transistor architecture where the gate surrounds the channel completely, providing maximum electrostatic control.

## Evolution

```
Planar MOSFET
      ↓
FinFET
      ↓
GAAFET
      ↓
Nanosheet FET
```

## Structure

The gate surrounds the channel on all sides.

Benefits:

- Excellent gate control
- Reduced leakage
- Better scaling capability

## Nanosheet Technology

Modern GAAFET implementations use stacked nanosheets to improve drive current.

## Advantages

- Improved performance
- Lower power consumption
- Supports advanced process nodes

## Challenges

- Manufacturing complexity
- Precise nanosheet control
- Higher fabrication cost

## Applications

- Advanced CPUs
- AI processors
- High-performance SoCs

## Industry Relevance

GAAFET is used in next-generation semiconductor processes targeting beyond FinFET scaling.
