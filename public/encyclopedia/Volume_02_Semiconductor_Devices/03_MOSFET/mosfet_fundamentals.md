# MOSFET Fundamentals

## Overview

The Metal Oxide Semiconductor Field Effect Transistor (MOSFET) is the most important semiconductor device in modern electronics. Almost every CPU, GPU, memory chip and SoC is built using billions of MOSFETs.

## Prerequisites

- Semiconductor physics
- PN junction
- Energy bands
- Carrier transport
- Capacitance

## Structure

A MOSFET consists of:

- Gate
- Oxide layer
- Semiconductor channel
- Source
- Drain

## Operating Principle

The electric field created by the gate voltage controls the conductivity of the channel between source and drain.

## Types

- NMOS
- PMOS
- CMOS

## Operating Regions

### Cutoff

MOSFET behaves as OFF switch.

### Linear Region

Used for voltage-controlled resistance operation.

### Saturation Region

Used for amplification and current source operation.

## Applications

- Digital logic
- Processors
- Memory
- Analog ICs
- Power electronics

## Industry Importance

MOSFET technology evolved from planar transistor to FinFET and GAAFET for advanced semiconductor nodes.
