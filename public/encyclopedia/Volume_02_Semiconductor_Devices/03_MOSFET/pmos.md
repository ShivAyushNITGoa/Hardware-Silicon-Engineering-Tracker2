# PMOS Transistor

## Overview
PMOS (P-channel MOSFET) is a MOS transistor where holes are the main charge carriers.

## Structure
- N-type substrate
- P+ source and drain
- Gate oxide
- Metal gate

## Operation
A negative gate voltage creates a hole inversion channel between source and drain.

## Characteristics
- Lower mobility than NMOS
- Used together with NMOS in CMOS technology
- Provides pull-up networks

## Applications
- CMOS logic
- Power switches
- Analog circuits

## Comparison
| Feature | NMOS | PMOS |
|---|---|---|
| Carrier | Electrons | Holes |
| Mobility | Higher | Lower |
| Speed | Faster | Slower |
| Logic use | Pull-down | Pull-up |
