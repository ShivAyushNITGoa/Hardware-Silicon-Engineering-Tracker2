# MOSFET Threshold Voltage

## Definition
Threshold voltage (VTH) is the minimum gate voltage required to create an inversion channel.

## Factors Affecting VTH
- Substrate doping
- Oxide thickness
- Gate material
- Temperature
- Body bias

## Importance
VTH affects:
- Switching speed
- Power consumption
- Leakage current
- CMOS performance

## Modern Technology
As transistor dimensions shrink, controlling threshold voltage becomes critical because of leakage and variability.

## Related Concepts
- Body effect
- Short channel effects
- Low power design
