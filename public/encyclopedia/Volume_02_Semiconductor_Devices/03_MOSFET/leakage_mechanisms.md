# MOSFET Leakage Mechanisms

## Overview
As transistor dimensions shrink, unwanted current paths become a major challenge in modern semiconductor devices.

## Types of Leakage

### Subthreshold Leakage
- Current flowing when MOSFET is OFF
- Caused by weak inversion conduction
- Important in low-power design

### Gate Leakage
- Quantum tunneling through thin oxide
- Reduced using high-k dielectric materials

### Junction Leakage
- Leakage through source/drain junctions

### Gate Induced Drain Leakage (GIDL)
- High electric field near drain region
- Important in advanced nodes

## Impact
- Increased standby power
- Battery life reduction
- Thermal problems

## Reduction Techniques
- Multi-threshold CMOS
- Power gating
- FinFET structures
- High-k metal gate technology

## Applications
- Mobile processors
- AI accelerators
- Low-power SoCs
