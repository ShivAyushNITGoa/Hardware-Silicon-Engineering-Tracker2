# MOSFET Scaling

## Introduction
Scaling reduces transistor dimensions to improve performance, density, and power efficiency.

## Scaling Generations

```text
Micrometer MOSFET
        ↓
Planar Nanometer MOSFET
        ↓
FinFET
        ↓
GAAFET
        ↓
Nanosheet
```

## Benefits

- Higher transistor density
- Faster switching
- Lower operating voltage
- More computing capability

## Challenges

- Leakage current
- Short channel effects
- Heat generation
- Manufacturing complexity

## Modern Technologies

### High-k Metal Gate
Reduces gate leakage.

### FinFET
Improves electrostatic control.

### GAAFET
Provides gate control around entire channel.

## Industry Usage

Used in:
- CPUs
- GPUs
- AI accelerators
- Mobile SoCs
