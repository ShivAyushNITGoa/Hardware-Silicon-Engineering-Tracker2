# MOSFET Short Channel Effects

## Introduction

When transistor dimensions reduce to nanometer scale, ideal MOSFET behaviour changes. These effects are called short channel effects.

## Major Effects

## Drain Induced Barrier Lowering (DIBL)

High drain voltage reduces source-channel barrier and increases leakage current.

## Threshold Voltage Roll-Off

Threshold voltage decreases as channel length reduces.

## Velocity Saturation

Carrier velocity stops increasing linearly with electric field.

## Hot Carrier Effect

High-energy carriers damage oxide and reduce reliability.

## Gate Leakage

Ultra-thin oxide allows tunneling current.

## Industry Solutions

- High-k metal gate
- Strained silicon
- FinFET
- GAAFET
- Nanosheet transistors

## Importance

Short channel effects determine modern semiconductor scaling limits.
