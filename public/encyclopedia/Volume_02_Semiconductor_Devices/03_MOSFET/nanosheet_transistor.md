# Nanosheet Transistor (Nanosheet FET)

## Overview
Nanosheet transistor is an advanced Gate-All-Around (GAA) device technology developed to continue transistor scaling beyond FinFET.

## Evolution

Planar MOSFET → FinFET → GAAFET → Nanosheet FET

## Structure

- Stacked horizontal silicon nanosheets
- Gate completely surrounds each sheet
- Multiple channels provide improved current control

## Advantages

- Better electrostatic control
- Lower leakage
- Higher drive current
- Improved scaling capability

## Challenges

- Complex fabrication
- Precise nanosheet thickness control
- Increased process complexity

## Industry Usage

Used in advanced semiconductor nodes for:

- High-performance CPUs
- AI accelerators
- Advanced SoCs

## Related Concepts

- Gate-All-Around FET
- EUV lithography
- Advanced CMOS nodes
