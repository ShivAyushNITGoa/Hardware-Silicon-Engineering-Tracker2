# Photodiode

## 1. Overview

A photodiode converts incident light into electrical current.

## 2. Prerequisites

- PN junction
- Carrier generation
- Semiconductor materials

## 3. Operation

Photons with energy greater than the band gap create electron-hole pairs. The junction electric field separates carriers and produces photocurrent.

## 4. Types

- PN photodiode
- PIN photodiode
- Avalanche photodiode

## 5. Parameters

- Responsivity
- Quantum efficiency
- Dark current
- Response time

## 6. Applications

- Cameras
- Optical communication
- LiDAR
- Sensors
