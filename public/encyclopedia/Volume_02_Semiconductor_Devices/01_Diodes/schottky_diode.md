# Schottky Diode

## Overview

A Schottky diode uses a metal-semiconductor junction instead of a PN junction.

## Key Characteristics

- Very low forward voltage
- Fast switching speed
- Low reverse recovery time

## Structure

Metal contact + Semiconductor

## Applications

- High frequency circuits
- Power supplies
- RF detectors
- Switching converters

## Comparison

| Parameter | PN Diode | Schottky |
|---|---|---|
| Junction | PN | Metal-semiconductor |
| Speed | Medium | High |
| Forward voltage | Higher | Lower |

- High speed rectifier simulation
