# BJT Comparison Summary

## NPN vs PNP

| Feature | NPN | PNP |
|---|---|---|
| Main carrier | Electrons | Holes |
| Mobility | Higher | Lower |
| Conventional current | Collector to emitter | Emitter to collector |
| Common usage | Low-side switching | High-side switching |

## BJT vs MOSFET

| Feature | BJT | MOSFET |
|---|---|---|
| Control | Current controlled | Voltage controlled |
| Input impedance | Lower | Very high |
| Switching speed | Moderate | Higher |
| Power consumption | Higher | Lower |
| Main use | Analog/RF | Digital ICs |

## BJT vs CMOS

CMOS dominates modern processors because of:

- Low power
- High density
- Scalability
- Large integration capability

BJT remains important in:

- Precision analog
- RF circuits
- BiCMOS technology
