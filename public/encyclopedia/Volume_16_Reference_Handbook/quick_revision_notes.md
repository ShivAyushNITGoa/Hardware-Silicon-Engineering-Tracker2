# Semiconductor Quick Revision Notes

## Semiconductor Basics

- Semiconductor: Material with conductivity between conductor and insulator.
- Silicon is the most widely used semiconductor material.
- Band gap determines semiconductor behavior.

## Transistor Quick Reference

### MOSFET

- NMOS: Electron conduction.
- PMOS: Hole conduction.
- CMOS: Combination of NMOS and PMOS.

Important parameters:

- Threshold voltage
- Mobility
- Leakage current
- Drive current
