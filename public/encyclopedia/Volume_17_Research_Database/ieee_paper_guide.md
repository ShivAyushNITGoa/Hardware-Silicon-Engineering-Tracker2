# IEEE Paper Guide

## Research Paper Workflow

1. Identify research area
2. Search IEEE Xplore and journals
3. Read abstract and introduction
4. Study methodology
5. Analyze results
6. Extract research gaps

## Important Semiconductor Research Areas

- Advanced transistor technology
- VLSI architecture
- AI accelerators
- Semiconductor fabrication
- Advanced packaging
- Low power design

## Paper Reading Strategy

- Understand problem statement
- Study previous approaches
- Analyze proposed improvement
- Compare performance metrics
- Note future scope

## Key Metrics

- Power
- Performance
- Area
- Energy efficiency
- Yield
- Reliability
