# Cadence EDA Tools

## Overview

Cadence is one of the largest semiconductor EDA providers.

## Major Tools

### Virtuoso

Used for:

- Analog IC design
- Custom layout
- CMOS design
- RF circuits

### Innovus

Used for:

- Digital physical design
- Place and route
- Timing closure

### Spectre

Used for:

- Circuit simulation
- Analog verification

### Xcelium

Used for:

- Digital simulation
- SystemVerilog verification

## Industry Usage

Used in:

- Qualcomm
- NVIDIA
- AMD
- TSMC ecosystem
- Semiconductor research labs
