# TCAD Tools

## Overview
Technology Computer-Aided Design (TCAD) simulates semiconductor fabrication processes and device physics.

## Major TCAD Activities

- Process simulation
- Device simulation
- Electrical characterization
- Reliability analysis

## Industry Tools

### Synopsys Sentaurus

Used for:
- CMOS device simulation
- FinFET/GAAFET analysis
- Process modeling
- Carrier transport simulation

### Silvaco TCAD

Includes:
- ATHENA process simulator
- ATLAS device simulator

Applications:
- MOSFET optimization
- Solar cells
- Power devices

## Concepts

- Doping profiles
- Mobility models
- Threshold voltage extraction
- Breakdown analysis
- Quantum effects
