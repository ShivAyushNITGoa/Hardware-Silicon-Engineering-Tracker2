# Low Power Design Methodology

## Overview
Modern semiconductor design focuses on minimizing power while maintaining performance.

## Power Components

- Dynamic power
- Leakage power
- Short circuit power

## Techniques

- Clock gating
- Power gating
- DVFS
- Multi-Vt cells
- Voltage scaling

## Applications

- Mobile SoCs
- IoT chips
- Wearable devices
- Data center processors

## Verification

Checks:

- Power estimation
- Thermal behavior
- Battery constraints
