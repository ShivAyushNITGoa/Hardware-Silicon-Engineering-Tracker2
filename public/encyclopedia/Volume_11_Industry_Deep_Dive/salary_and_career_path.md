# Semiconductor Career Path

## Entry Level

Roles:
- Junior RTL Engineer
- Verification Engineer
- Embedded Engineer

Skills:
- Digital logic
- Programming
- HDL basics

## Mid Level

Roles:
- Design Engineer
- Senior Verification Engineer
- Physical Design Engineer

Skills:
- Complete design flow
- Optimization
- Debugging

## Advanced

Roles:
- SoC Architect
- Principal Engineer
- Research Engineer

Skills:
- Architecture
- Innovation
- System-level design

Growth areas:
- AI hardware
- Chiplets
- Advanced packaging
- Semiconductor manufacturing
