# Ion Implantation

## Overview
Ion implantation is a semiconductor doping process used to precisely introduce impurities into silicon.

## Purpose
- Control transistor threshold voltage
- Create source and drain regions
- Adjust device characteristics

## Process

```text
Dopant Source
 ↓
Ion Generation
 ↓
Acceleration
 ↓
Mass Separation
 ↓
Wafer Implantation
 ↓
Annealing
```

## Common Dopants

| Dopant | Type |
|---|---|
| Boron | P-type |
| Phosphorus | N-type |
| Arsenic | N-type |

## Parameters

- Implant dose
- Implant energy
- Depth profile
- Activation efficiency

## Applications

- MOSFET fabrication
- CMOS wells
- Advanced transistor engineering
