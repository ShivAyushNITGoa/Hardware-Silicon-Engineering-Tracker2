# Diffusion Process

## Overview
Diffusion is a thermal process used to move dopant atoms into silicon.

## Mechanism

Dopants move from high concentration regions to low concentration regions using thermal energy.

## Types

- Pre-deposition
- Drive-in diffusion

## Parameters

- Temperature
- Time
- Dopant concentration
- Diffusion coefficient

## Comparison

| Process | Control | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Diffusion | Thermal movement | Older processes |
| Ion implantation | Ion beam | Modern CMOS |
