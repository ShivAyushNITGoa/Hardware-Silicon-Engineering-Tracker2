# Semiconductor Manufacturing Process Fundamentals

## Overview

A semiconductor fabrication process converts pure silicon into billions of transistor-based integrated circuits.

## Complete Process Flow

1. Silicon purification
2. Wafer preparation
3. Oxidation
4. Photolithography
5. Etching
6. Deposition
7. Ion implantation
8. Annealing
9. CMP
10. Metallization
11. Testing
12. Packaging

## Fabrication Philosophy

Modern fabs optimize:

- Performance
- Power
- Area
- Yield
- Reliability
