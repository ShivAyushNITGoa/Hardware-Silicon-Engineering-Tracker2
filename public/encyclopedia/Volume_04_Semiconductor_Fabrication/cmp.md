# Chemical Mechanical Planarization (CMP)

## Overview
CMP is used to flatten wafer surfaces during semiconductor fabrication.

## Purpose

- Remove excess material
- Create planar surfaces
- Enable multiple interconnect layers

## Process

```text
Chemical Slurry
+
Mechanical Polishing
=
Surface Planarization
```

## Applications

- Multilevel metal interconnects
- Advanced CMOS fabrication
- Memory manufacturing
