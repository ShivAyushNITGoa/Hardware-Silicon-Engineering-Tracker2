# Semiconductor Etching

## Overview

Etching removes selected material to create semiconductor structures.

## Types

### Wet Etching

- Chemical solutions
- High selectivity
- Isotropic removal

### Dry Etching

- Plasma based
- High precision
- Used in advanced nodes

## Plasma Etching

Processes:

- Reactive Ion Etching (RIE)
- Deep Reactive Ion Etching (DRIE)

## Applications

- Gate formation
- Interconnect patterning
- FinFET structures
