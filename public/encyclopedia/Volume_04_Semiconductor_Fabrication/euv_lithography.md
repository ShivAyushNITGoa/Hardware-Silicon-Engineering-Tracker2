# EUV Lithography

## Overview

Extreme Ultraviolet Lithography uses 13.5 nm wavelength light for advanced semiconductor manufacturing.

## Why EUV?

Required for:

- 7nm
- 5nm
- 3nm
- Future nodes

## Components

- EUV source
- Multilayer mirrors
- Reflective masks
- Vacuum chamber
- Photoresist

## Challenges

- Low power efficiency
- Defect control
- Mask complexity
- High equipment cost

## Industry

Main equipment supplier:

- ASML

Used by:

- TSMC
- Intel
- Samsung
