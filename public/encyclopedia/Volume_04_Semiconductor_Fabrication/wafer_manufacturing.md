# Wafer Manufacturing

## Silicon Source

Semiconductor wafers start from high-purity silicon.

## Major Steps

### 1. Crystal Growth

- Czochralski (CZ) process
- Single crystal silicon formation

### 2. Ingot Formation

Large cylindrical silicon crystal.

### 3. Wafer Slicing

Diamond saw cuts thin wafers.

### 4. Polishing

Creates atomically smooth surface.

### 5. Cleaning

Removes contaminants.

## Important Parameters

- Wafer diameter
- Defect density
- Surface roughness
- Crystal orientation

## Industry Sizes

- 150 mm
- 200 mm
- 300 mm wafers
