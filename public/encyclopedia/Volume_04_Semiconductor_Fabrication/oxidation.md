# Silicon Oxidation

## Overview
Oxidation creates silicon dioxide (SiO2) layers on silicon wafers.

## Types

### Dry Oxidation
- Higher quality oxide
- Used for thin gate oxide

### Wet Oxidation
- Faster growth
- Used for thicker oxide layers

## Applications

- Gate dielectric
- Isolation structures
- Surface protection

## Importance
SiO2 was one of the foundations of MOS technology.
