# Semiconductor Salary and Career Progression

## Career Stages

### Fresher (0-2 years)

Focus:
- Fundamentals
- Tool knowledge
- Project experience

### Engineer (2-5 years)

Focus:
- Design ownership
- Debugging
- Optimization

### Senior Engineer (5-10 years)

Focus:
- Architecture decisions
- Mentoring
- Technical leadership

### Principal / Architect

Focus:
- System-level design
- Research direction
- Technology strategy

## Growth Factors

Important factors:
- Technical depth
- Industry specialization
- Project impact
- Continuous learning
- Leadership skills

## High Growth Areas

- AI accelerators
- Advanced packaging
- Chiplet architecture
- Semiconductor manufacturing
- EDA automation
