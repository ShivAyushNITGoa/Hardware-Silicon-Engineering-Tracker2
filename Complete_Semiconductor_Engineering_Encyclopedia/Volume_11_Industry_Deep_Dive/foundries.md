# Semiconductor Foundries

## Definition

Foundries manufacture semiconductor chips designed by other companies.

## Major Foundries

### TSMC

Skills:
- Advanced CMOS processes
- Process technology
- Yield engineering
- Manufacturing

### Samsung Foundry

Skills:
- Logic process
- Memory integration
- Advanced packaging

### Intel Foundry

Skills:
- Process technology
- Chip manufacturing
- Packaging

## Foundry Workflow

Wafer fabrication → Testing → Packaging → Production
