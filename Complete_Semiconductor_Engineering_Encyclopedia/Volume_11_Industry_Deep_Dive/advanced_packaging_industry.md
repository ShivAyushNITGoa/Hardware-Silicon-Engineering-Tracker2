# Advanced Packaging Industry

## Why Advanced Packaging

Modern chips use packaging to improve:
- Performance
- Bandwidth
- Power efficiency
- Integration

## Technologies

## 2.5D Integration

Uses:
- Interposers
- Multiple dies

## 3D IC

Uses:
- Vertical stacking
- TSV connections

## Chiplets

Concept:

Small specialized dies combined into one system.

Applications:
- CPUs
- GPUs
- AI accelerators

## HBM

Features:
- High bandwidth memory
- 3D stacked DRAM
- AI/HPC applications
