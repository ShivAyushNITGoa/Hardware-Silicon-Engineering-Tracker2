# Analog IC Design Flow

## Overview
Analog design converts specifications into transistor-level circuits and physical layouts.

## Flow

Specification
↓
Architecture
↓
Circuit Design
↓
Simulation
↓
Layout
↓
DRC/LVS
↓
Fabrication

## Major Steps

- Requirements analysis
- Device selection
- Bias design
- Schematic design
- SPICE simulation
- Layout optimization
- Post-layout verification

## Tools

- Cadence Virtuoso
- Spectre
- LTspice
- Synopsys Custom Compiler

## Applications

- ADC/DAC
- PLL
- RF circuits
- PMIC
- Sensor interfaces
