# Semiconductor Yield Engineering

## Overview

Yield engineering focuses on improving the percentage of working chips produced from wafers.

## Factors Affecting Yield

- Defects
- Process variation
- Equipment stability
- Contamination

## Techniques

- Statistical process control
- Defect analysis
- Failure analysis
- Process optimization

## Importance

High yield reduces manufacturing cost and improves semiconductor production efficiency.

## Applications

- Advanced logic fabs
- Memory fabs
- Packaging facilities
