# Lithography in Semiconductor Fabrication

## Overview

Photolithography transfers circuit patterns from a mask onto a silicon wafer.

## Basic Process

1. Wafer cleaning
2. Photoresist coating
3. Soft bake
4. Mask alignment
5. UV/EUV exposure
6. Development
7. Pattern transfer

## Types

- Optical lithography
- Deep UV (DUV)
- Extreme UV (EUV)
- Electron beam lithography

## Important Parameters

- Resolution
- Overlay accuracy
- Critical dimension (CD)
- Depth of focus
- Exposure dose

## Applications

Used for creating:

- Transistor gates
- Interconnect layers
- Memory structures
- Advanced logic nodes
