# Routing in ASIC Physical Design

## Overview
Routing connects placed standard cells, macros, power structures and clock networks using metal layers.

## Stages

- Global routing
- Detailed routing
- Optimization

## Concepts

- Metal layers
- Vias
- Wire resistance
- Wire capacitance
- Congestion
- Crosstalk

## Goals

- Meet timing
- Reduce delay
- Reduce power
- Ensure manufacturability
