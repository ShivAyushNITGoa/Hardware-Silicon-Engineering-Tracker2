# Placement in VLSI

## Overview
Placement assigns physical locations to standard cells after synthesis.

## Goals
- Timing optimization
- Congestion reduction
- Area optimization
- Better routability

## Stages
- Global placement
- Legalization
- Detailed placement

## Optimization
- Cell resizing
- Buffer insertion
- Logic restructuring

## Tools
- Cadence Innovus
- Synopsys ICC2
- OpenROAD
