# Analog IC Design Fundamentals

## Introduction

Analog IC design deals with continuous-time signals and circuits used for sensing, amplification, conversion, and communication.

## Core Concepts

- Voltage and current signals
- Biasing
- Small signal analysis
- Gain
- Bandwidth
- Noise
- Linearity
- Stability

## Basic Analog Building Blocks

- Current source
- Current mirror
- Differential pair
- Common source amplifier
- Common drain amplifier
- Operational amplifier

## Applications

- ADC/DAC
- RF circuits
- Sensors
- Power management ICs
- Communication systems
