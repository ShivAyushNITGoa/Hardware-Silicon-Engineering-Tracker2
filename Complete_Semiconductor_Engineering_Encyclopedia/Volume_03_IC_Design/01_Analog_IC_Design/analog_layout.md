# Analog IC Layout

## Overview
Analog layout converts circuit schematics into physical geometry while maintaining electrical performance.

## Important Concepts
- Matching
- Symmetry
- Parasitics
- Common centroid layout
- Guard rings
- Shielding
- Dummy devices

## Layout Effects
- Resistance
- Capacitance
- Noise coupling
- Process variation

## Analog Layout Techniques

### Common Centroid
Used for matched devices such as differential pairs and current mirrors.

### Interdigitation
Alternating device placement to reduce gradient errors.

### Guard Rings
Prevent substrate noise coupling.
