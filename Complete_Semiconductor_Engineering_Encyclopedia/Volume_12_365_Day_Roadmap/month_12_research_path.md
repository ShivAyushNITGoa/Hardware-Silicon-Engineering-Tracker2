# Month 12 — Semiconductor Research Path

## Goal

Transition from engineering fundamentals to semiconductor research and innovation.

## Week 1 — Research Fundamentals

Topics:

- Reading IEEE papers
- Understanding technical publications
- Literature survey
- Research problem identification

## Week 2 — Advanced Semiconductor Research Areas

Topics:

- Advanced transistor architectures
- GAAFET and nanosheet devices
- 3D IC and chiplets
- Advanced packaging
- AI accelerator architectures

## Week 3 — Practical Research Projects

Projects:

- MOSFET device simulation
- RISC-V processor optimization
- AI hardware accelerator design
- Low-power circuit design
- Open-source ASIC tapeout

Tools:

- TCAD
- SPICE
- Verilog/SystemVerilog
- OpenROAD
- Python for analysis

## Week 4 — Research Career Preparation

Activities:

- Build technical portfolio
- Publish documentation
- Contribute to open-source projects
- Participate in semiconductor communities

## Research Career Paths

- Semiconductor device researcher
- VLSI researcher
- Architecture researcher
- Process technology researcher
- AI hardware researcher

## Final Outcome

After completing 12 months, the learner should have knowledge of:

- Semiconductor physics
- Electronics
- RTL design
- Verification
- FPGA
- ASIC flow
- Fabrication
- Industry practices
- Research methodology
