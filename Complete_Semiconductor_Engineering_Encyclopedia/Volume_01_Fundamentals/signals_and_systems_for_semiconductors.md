# Signals and Systems for Semiconductor Engineering

## Purpose
Foundation of signals used in semiconductor circuits, communication blocks, and SoC systems.

## Topics

- Continuous and discrete signals
- Time domain and frequency domain analysis
- Sampling theory
- Fourier transform basics
- Laplace transform basics
- Noise and signal integrity

## Semiconductor Applications

- ADC/DAC design
- Clock generation
- High-speed interfaces
- RF circuits
- Sensor interfaces

## Key Concepts

- Bandwidth
- Rise time
- Delay
- Frequency response
- SNR
