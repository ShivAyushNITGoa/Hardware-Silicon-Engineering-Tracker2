# Carrier Concentration

## Prerequisites
- Energy bands
- Electrons and holes
- Semiconductor materials

## Overview
Carrier concentration describes the number of charge carriers available per unit volume in a semiconductor.

## Intrinsic Semiconductor

A pure semiconductor where:

- Electron concentration equals hole concentration
- n = p = ni

## Extrinsic Semiconductor

Doping changes carrier concentration.

### N-type

Majority carriers:
- Electrons

Dopants:
- Phosphorus
- Arsenic

### P-type

Majority carriers:
- Holes

Dopants:
- Boron
- Gallium

## Important Equation

Mass action law:

n × p = ni²

## Importance
Carrier concentration affects:

- Conductivity
- Threshold voltage
- Device speed
- Leakage
