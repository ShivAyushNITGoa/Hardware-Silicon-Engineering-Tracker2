# Fermi Level

## 1. Prerequisites

- Atomic structure
- Quantum mechanics basics
- Energy bands
- Semiconductor materials

## 2. Overview

The Fermi level is the energy level at which the probability of finding an electron is 50% at thermal equilibrium.

It is one of the most important concepts for understanding semiconductor behaviour.

## 3. Energy Distribution

Electrons occupy energy states according to Fermi-Dirac statistics.

The Fermi-Dirac distribution is:

```
f(E)=1/(1+e^((E-Ef)/kT))
```

Where:

- Ef = Fermi energy
- k = Boltzmann constant
- T = Temperature

## 4. Fermi Level in Materials

### Intrinsic Semiconductor

The Fermi level lies near the middle of the band gap.

### N-Type Semiconductor

Doping adds donor atoms. The Fermi level moves closer to the conduction band.

### P-Type Semiconductor

Doping adds acceptor atoms. The Fermi level moves closer to the valence band.

## 5. Importance

The Fermi level determines:

- Carrier concentration
- Electrical conductivity
- Junction behaviour
- Device operation
