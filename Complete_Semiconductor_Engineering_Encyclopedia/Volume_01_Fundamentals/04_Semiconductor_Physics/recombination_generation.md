# Carrier Generation and Recombination

## 1. Overview
Generation creates electron-hole pairs. Recombination removes free carriers by combining electrons and holes.

## 2. Prerequisites
- Energy bands
- Carrier concentration
- Semiconductor physics

## 3. Generation
Types:
- Thermal generation
- Optical generation
- Electrical generation

## 4. Recombination Mechanisms
### Radiative recombination
Electron releases energy as a photon.

Applications:
- LEDs
- Lasers

### Shockley-Read-Hall recombination
Occurs through defects and traps.

### Auger recombination
Energy is transferred to another carrier.

## 5. Importance
Controls:
- Device speed
- Leakage current
- Solar cell efficiency
- LED brightness

## 6. Industry Applications
- Image sensors
- Photonics
- Memory devices
- Power electronics
