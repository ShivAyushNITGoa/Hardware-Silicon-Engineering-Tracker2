# Carrier Transport in Semiconductors

## 1. Prerequisites

- Energy bands
- Electrons and holes
- Semiconductor materials
- Electric fields

## 2. Overview

Carrier transport describes how electrons and holes move inside semiconductor materials.

The two primary mechanisms are:

1. Drift
2. Diffusion

## 3. Drift Current

Drift occurs due to an electric field.

Electron movement:

- Opposite direction of electric field

Hole movement:

- Same direction as electric field

Current density:

```
J = q n μ E
```

Where:

- q = electron charge
- n = carrier concentration
- μ = mobility
- E = electric field

## 4. Diffusion Current

Diffusion occurs because of carrier concentration differences.

Carriers move from high concentration to low concentration regions.

Diffusion is responsible for PN junction operation.

## 5. Mobility

Mobility represents how easily carriers move through a semiconductor.

Affected by:

- Temperature
- Crystal defects
- Doping concentration

## 6. Industry Importance

Carrier transport affects:

- Transistor speed
- Leakage current
- Power consumption
- Device scaling

## 7. Applications

Important in:

- MOSFET design
- Solar cells
- Sensors
- Power devices
