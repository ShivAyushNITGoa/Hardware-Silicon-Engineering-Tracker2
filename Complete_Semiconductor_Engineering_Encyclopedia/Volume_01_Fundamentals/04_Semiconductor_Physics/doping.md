# Semiconductor Doping

## 1. Prerequisites

- Atomic structure
- Energy bands
- Silicon crystal structure

## 2. Overview

Doping is the intentional addition of impurities into a semiconductor to control electrical properties.

Pure silicon has limited conductivity. Doping creates useful semiconductor materials.

## 3. Types of Doping

## N-Type Semiconductor

Created by adding donor impurities.

Common dopants:

- Phosphorus
- Arsenic

Major carriers:

- Electrons

## P-Type Semiconductor

Created by adding acceptor impurities.

Common dopants:

- Boron
- Gallium

Major carriers:

- Holes

## 4. Effect of Doping

Changes:

- Carrier concentration
- Conductivity
- Fermi level position
- Junction behaviour

## 5. Applications

Doping is used in:

- Diodes
- MOSFETs
- IC fabrication
- Solar cells
- Power devices

## 6. Manufacturing Methods

- Ion implantation
- Thermal diffusion
