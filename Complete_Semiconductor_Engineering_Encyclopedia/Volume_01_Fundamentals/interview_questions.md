# Volume 01 Fundamentals Interview Questions

## Semiconductor Physics

### Q1. What is a semiconductor?

A material whose electrical conductivity lies between conductor and insulator. Conductivity can be controlled by doping.

### Q2. Why is silicon used in IC manufacturing?

Reasons:
- Suitable band gap
- High quality oxide formation
- Abundant material
- Mature manufacturing process

### Q3. Difference between intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductor?

Intrinsic:
- Pure semiconductor
- Equal electrons and holes

Extrinsic:
- Doped semiconductor
- Controlled carrier concentration

### Q4. Explain Fermi level.

The Fermi level represents the energy level where the probability of electron occupation is 50% at thermal equilibrium.

### Q5. Difference between drift and diffusion current?

Drift:
- Caused by electric field

Diffusion:
- Caused by carrier concentration gradient

## Device Questions

### Q6. What is band gap?

Energy difference between valence band and conduction band.

### Q7. Why does doping increase conductivity?

Because it increases free carrier concentration.

### Q8. Explain PN junction formation.

Formation occurs when P-type and N-type materials join, creating a depletion region and built-in potential.
