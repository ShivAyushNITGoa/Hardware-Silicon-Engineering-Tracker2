# Chiplet Research

## Overview
Chiplet architecture separates a complex SoC into smaller reusable dies connected through advanced interconnects.

## Research Areas
- Advanced packaging
- Die-to-die communication
- 2.5D and 3D integration
- Heterogeneous integration
- Memory integration

## Technologies
- Silicon interposer
- Advanced interconnects
- UCIe ecosystem
- High bandwidth memory integration

## Challenges
- Thermal management
- Communication latency
- Manufacturing yield
- Verification complexity
