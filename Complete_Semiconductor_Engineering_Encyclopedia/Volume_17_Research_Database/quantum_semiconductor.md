# Quantum Semiconductor Research

## Introduction

Quantum semiconductor research explores devices and systems based on quantum effects.

## Research Areas

- Quantum dots
- Quantum wells
- Semiconductor qubits
- Single photon devices
- Quantum materials

## Device Technologies

- III-V semiconductors
- Silicon quantum devices
- Heterostructures

## Challenges

- Noise reduction
- Manufacturing complexity
- Device stability
- Scaling quantum systems

## Future Applications

- Quantum computing
- Secure communication
- Advanced sensing
