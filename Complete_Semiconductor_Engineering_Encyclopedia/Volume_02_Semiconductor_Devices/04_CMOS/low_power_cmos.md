# Low Power CMOS

## Need for Low Power Design

Modern chips face power and thermal limitations.

## Power Components

### Dynamic Power

Caused by charging and discharging capacitances.

P = αCV²f

### Static Power

Caused by leakage currents.

## Techniques

- Power gating
- Clock gating
- Voltage scaling
- Multi-threshold CMOS
- Dynamic voltage frequency scaling

## Applications

- Mobile processors
- IoT chips
- Wearables
- AI accelerators
