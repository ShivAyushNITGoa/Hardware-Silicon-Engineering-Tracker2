# CMOS Clock Tree

## Overview

Clock distribution delivers clock signals to millions/billions of sequential elements.

## Clock Tree Components

- Clock source
- Clock buffers
- Clock routing network
- Clock sinks

## Important Problems

### Clock Skew
Difference in arrival time between clock signals.

### Clock Jitter
Variation caused by noise and instability.

### Clock Latency
Time taken for clock propagation.

## Optimization

- Buffer insertion
- Balanced routing
- Clock gating

## Applications

- CPUs
- GPUs
- SoCs
- ASICs
