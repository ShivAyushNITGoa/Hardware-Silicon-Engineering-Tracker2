# BJT Applications

## Overview

Bipolar Junction Transistors are used where high gain, accurate current control, and analog performance are required.

## Major Applications

### 1. Amplifiers

- Audio amplifiers
- RF amplifiers
- Signal conditioning circuits

### 2. Switching

BJT operates as an electronic switch:

- Cutoff region = OFF
- Saturation region = ON

Applications:
- Relay drivers
- LED drivers
- Motor control

### 3. Current Sources and Mirrors

Used extensively in analog IC bias networks.

### 4. Differential Amplifiers

Foundation of:
- Operational amplifiers
- Comparators
- Analog processors

### 5. Power Applications

- Power switching
- Motor drives
- Power regulation

## Industry Usage

BJT technology is still used in:

- Analog ICs
- RF circuits
- Bipolar CMOS (BiCMOS) processes
- High-performance amplifiers
