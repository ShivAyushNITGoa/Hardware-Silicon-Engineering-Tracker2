# BJT Biasing

## Purpose

Biasing establishes the correct operating point (Q-point) of a transistor.

## Types of Biasing

### Fixed Bias

- Simple circuit
- Poor temperature stability

### Collector Feedback Bias

- Improved stability

### Voltage Divider Bias

- Most commonly used
- Better thermal stability

## Operating Point

The Q-point defines:

- Collector current IC
- Collector-emitter voltage VCE

## Biasing Effects

Temperature changes affect:

- Leakage current
- Gain
- Stability

## Applications

- Amplifier design
- Analog circuits
- Integrated circuits
