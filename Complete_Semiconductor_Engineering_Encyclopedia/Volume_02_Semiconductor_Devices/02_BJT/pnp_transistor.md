# PNP Transistor

## 1. Structure

PNP transistor consists of:

- P-type emitter
- N-type base
- P-type collector

## 2. Operation

Holes are the majority carriers.

Emitter-base junction is forward biased and collector-base junction is reverse biased during active operation.

## 3. Characteristics

Similar to NPN but current direction and polarity are reversed.

## 4. Applications

- High-side switching
- Analog circuits
- Complementary transistor pairs

## 5. Comparison with NPN

NPN:
- Electron conduction
- Higher mobility
- More common

PNP:
- Hole conduction
- Opposite polarity operation
