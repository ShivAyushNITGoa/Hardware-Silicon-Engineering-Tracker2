# NPN Transistor

## 1. Structure

NPN transistor consists of:

- N-type emitter
- P-type base
- N-type collector

## 2. Operation

Forward biasing emitter-base junction injects electrons into the base region.

Most electrons reach the collector due to reverse biased collector-base junction.

## 3. Characteristics

Important curves:

- Input characteristic
- Output characteristic
- Transfer characteristic

## 4. Equations

Collector current:

Ic = βIb

Emitter current:

Ie = Ic + Ib

## 5. Applications

- Amplifiers
- Current mirrors
- Switching
- Analog circuits

## 6. Comparison

Advantages:
- High transconductance
- Good analog performance

Disadvantages:
- Higher power consumption
- Slower switching than MOSFET in many applications
