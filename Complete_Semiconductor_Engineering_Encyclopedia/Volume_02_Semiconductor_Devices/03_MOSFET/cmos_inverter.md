# CMOS Inverter

## Overview
The CMOS inverter is the fundamental building block of digital integrated circuits.

## Structure

Contains:
- PMOS transistor (pull-up)
- NMOS transistor (pull-down)

## Operation

### Input LOW
- PMOS ON
- NMOS OFF
- Output HIGH

### Input HIGH
- PMOS OFF
- NMOS ON
- Output LOW

## Characteristics

- High noise margin
- Low static power
- High scalability

## Important Parameters

- Voltage transfer characteristic
- Switching threshold
- Propagation delay
- Power consumption

## Applications

- Logic gates
- CPUs
- GPUs
- Memory circuits
- SoCs

## Advanced Topics

- CMOS scaling
- Dynamic power
- Leakage power
- FinFET CMOS
