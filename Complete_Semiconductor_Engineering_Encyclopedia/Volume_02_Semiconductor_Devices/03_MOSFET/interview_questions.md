# MOSFET Interview Questions

## Fundamentals

- Explain MOSFET structure and operation.
- Difference between NMOS and PMOS.
- Why MOSFET is called a voltage controlled device?
- Explain cutoff, linear and saturation regions.

## Device Physics

- What is threshold voltage?
- Explain body effect.
- What causes short channel effects?
- Explain DIBL and velocity saturation.

## CMOS

- Explain CMOS inverter operation.
- Why CMOS consumes low static power?
- Explain noise margin and propagation delay.

## Advanced Technology

- Why did industry move from planar MOSFET to FinFET?
- Explain GAAFET advantages.
- Compare FinFET and nanosheet transistor.

## Design Questions

- How can leakage power be reduced?
- How does transistor sizing affect delay?
- Explain power-performance-area tradeoff.
