# MOSFET Body Effect

## Introduction

Body effect describes the change in threshold voltage when the source and body voltages are different.

## Cause

The substrate potential changes the depletion region width beneath the gate.

## Threshold Voltage Equation

VTH = VTH0 + γ(√(2ΦF + VSB) - √(2ΦF))

## Parameters

- VTH0: Zero body bias threshold voltage
- γ: Body effect coefficient
- ΦF: Surface potential
- VSB: Source-body voltage

## Impact

- Changes transistor speed
- Affects circuit timing
- Important in analog design

## Applications

- CMOS circuits
- Current mirrors
- Low-power design
