# MOSFET Device Comparison

## NMOS vs PMOS

| Feature | NMOS | PMOS |
|---|---|---|
| Carrier | Electrons | Holes |
| Mobility | Higher | Lower |
| Speed | Faster | Slower |
| CMOS Role | Pull-down | Pull-up |

## Planar MOSFET vs FinFET vs GAAFET

| Feature | Planar | FinFET | GAAFET |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gate control | Single side | Three sides | All sides |
| Leakage | High | Lower | Lowest |
| Scaling | Limited | Better | Advanced |
| Manufacturing | Easier | Complex | More complex |

## BJT vs MOSFET

| Feature | BJT | MOSFET |
|---|---|---|
| Control | Current | Voltage |
| Input impedance | Lower | Very high |
| Switching | Moderate | Very fast |
| Main usage | Analog/RF | Digital ICs |
