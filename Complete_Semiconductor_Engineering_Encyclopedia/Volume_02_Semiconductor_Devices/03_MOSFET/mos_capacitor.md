# MOS Capacitor

## Overview

The MOS capacitor is the foundation of MOSFET operation. Understanding it is essential before studying CMOS technology.

## Structure

Layers:

1. Metal gate
2. Oxide insulator
3. Semiconductor substrate

## Operating Modes

### Accumulation

Majority carriers gather near the oxide interface.

### Depletion

Mobile carriers are removed creating a depletion region.

### Inversion

Minority carriers form a conducting channel.

## Important Concepts

- Oxide capacitance
- Threshold voltage
- Surface potential
- Flat band voltage

## Applications

- MOSFET design
- Memory devices
- Analog ICs
- Sensor interfaces
