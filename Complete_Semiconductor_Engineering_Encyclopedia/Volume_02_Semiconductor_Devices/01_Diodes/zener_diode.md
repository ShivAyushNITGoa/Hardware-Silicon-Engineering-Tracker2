# Zener Diode

## 1. Overview

A Zener diode is a specially designed PN junction diode that operates in reverse breakdown to provide a stable voltage reference.

## 2. Prerequisites

- PN junction diode
- Semiconductor doping
- Breakdown mechanisms

## 3. Working Principle

When reverse voltage reaches the Zener voltage, breakdown occurs and current increases while voltage remains approximately constant.

## 4. Breakdown Mechanisms

### Zener Breakdown
- Heavy doping
- Thin depletion region
- Quantum tunneling effect

### Avalanche Breakdown
- Light doping
- Carrier multiplication

## 5. Applications

- Voltage regulation
- Protection circuits
- Reference voltage generation
- Surge protection
