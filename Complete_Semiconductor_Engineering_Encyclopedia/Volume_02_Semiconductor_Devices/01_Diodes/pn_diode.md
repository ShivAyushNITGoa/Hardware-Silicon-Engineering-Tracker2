# PN Junction Diode

## 1. Prerequisites

- Semiconductor physics
- Energy bands
- Doping
- Carrier transport

## 2. Overview

A PN junction diode is formed by joining P-type and N-type semiconductor regions. It is the fundamental semiconductor device used in rectification, protection, sensing, and optoelectronics.

## 3. Formation

When P and N regions are joined:

- Electrons diffuse from N side to P side
- Holes diffuse from P side to N side
- Depletion region forms
- Built-in electric field develops

## 4. Operating Modes

### Forward Bias

- Depletion width decreases
- Current increases exponentially

### Reverse Bias

- Depletion width increases
- Small leakage current flows

### Breakdown

Types:

- Zener breakdown
- Avalanche breakdown

## 5. Equation

Shockley diode equation:

I = Is(e^(Vd/nVt)-1)

Where:

- Is = saturation current
- n = ideality factor
- Vt = thermal voltage
