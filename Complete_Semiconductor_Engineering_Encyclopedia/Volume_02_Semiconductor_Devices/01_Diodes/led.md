# Light Emitting Diode (LED)

## 1. Overview

LED is a semiconductor device that converts electrical energy into light using radiative recombination.

## 2. Prerequisites

- PN junction
- Band gap
- Carrier recombination

## 3. Working Principle

Forward bias injects electrons and holes into the active region. Their recombination releases photons.

## 4. Materials

- GaAs
- GaP
- GaN
- InGaN

## 5. Parameters

- Forward voltage
- Wavelength
- Luminous efficiency
- Quantum efficiency

## 6. Applications

- Displays
- Lighting
- Optical communication
- Indicators

## 7. Projects

- LED driver circuit
- PWM brightness controller
- Optical transmitter
