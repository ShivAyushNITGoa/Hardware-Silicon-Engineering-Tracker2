# VLSI Formula Sheet

## Power
Dynamic power:
P = alpha * C * V^2 * f

Static power:
P = Leakage current × Voltage

## Timing
Slack = Required time - Arrival time

## CMOS
Delay depends on:
- Load capacitance
- Drive current
- Supply voltage

## Area
Area optimization methods:
- Cell sizing
- Logic optimization
- Technology scaling

## Frequency
Higher frequency requires:
- Lower delay
- Better timing closure
