# TSMC Process Technology Case Study

## Overview

TSMC is a leading semiconductor foundry focusing on advanced manufacturing technologies.

## Process Technology Evolution

- Planar CMOS
- FinFET
- Nanosheet/GAAFET direction
- Advanced nodes

## Manufacturing Concepts

- EUV lithography
- Process control
- Yield optimization
- Design technology co-optimization (DTCO)

## Engineering Lessons

- Process scaling requires device, design and manufacturing co-optimization.
- Yield is a critical factor in semiconductor manufacturing success.
