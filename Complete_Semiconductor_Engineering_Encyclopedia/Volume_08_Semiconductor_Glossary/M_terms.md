# M Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## MOSFET

- Metal Oxide Semiconductor Field Effect Transistor.
- Fundamental building block of modern ICs.

## MOS Capacitor

- Structure used to study semiconductor surface behavior.
- Foundation of MOS transistor operation.

## Mask

- Pattern template used in photolithography.
- Defines semiconductor structures.

## Metal Layer

- Conductive interconnect layer inside ICs.
- Used for signal and power routing.

## Microarchitecture

- Internal organization of a processor or SoC.
- Defines datapaths, control, and execution units.

## Monte Carlo Analysis

- Statistical simulation method.
- Used for variation and reliability analysis.
