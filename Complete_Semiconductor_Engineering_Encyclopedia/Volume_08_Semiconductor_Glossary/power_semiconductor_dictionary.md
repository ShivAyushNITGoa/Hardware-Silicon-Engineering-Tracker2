# Power Semiconductor Dictionary

## Overview
Power semiconductors handle high voltage, current and power conversion applications.

## MOSFET
Used for:
- Switching converters
- Motor drives
- Power management

## IGBT — Insulated Gate Bipolar Transistor
Combines MOS gate control with bipolar conduction.

Applications:
- EV inverters
- Industrial drives

## SiC — Silicon Carbide
Advantages:
- High breakdown voltage
- High temperature operation
- High switching speed

Applications:
- Electric vehicles
- Renewable energy

## GaN — Gallium Nitride
Advantages:
- High electron mobility
- Fast switching

Applications:
- Fast chargers
- RF power devices

## Power Device Parameters
- Breakdown voltage
- RDS(on)
- Switching loss
- Thermal resistance
