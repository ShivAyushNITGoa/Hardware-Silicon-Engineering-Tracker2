# O-Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## OpenLane
Open-source automated RTL-to-GDSII ASIC flow.

Includes:
- Yosys synthesis
- OpenROAD physical design
- Magic verification

## OpenROAD
Open-source physical design automation framework.

Used for:
- Floorplanning
- Placement
- CTS
- Routing

## OPC — Optical Proximity Correction
Lithography technique used to compensate for optical distortions.

Importance:
- Improves pattern accuracy
- Enables advanced nodes

## Oxidation
Process of forming silicon oxide layers on wafers.

Applications:
- Gate oxide
- Isolation
- Surface protection

## Oscillator
Circuit generating periodic signals.

Applications:
- Clocks
- PLLs
- Timing circuits
