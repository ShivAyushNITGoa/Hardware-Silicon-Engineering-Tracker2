# Semiconductor Glossary — A Terms

## A/D Converter (ADC)

An Analog-to-Digital Converter converts continuous analog signals into digital data.

Applications:
- Sensor interfaces
- Communication systems
- Data acquisition

Related:
DAC, sampling, quantization

---

## ALD (Atomic Layer Deposition)

A thin-film deposition technique that deposits material one atomic layer at a time.

Applications:
- High-k dielectrics
- Gate stacks
- Advanced semiconductor nodes

---

## ASIC (Application Specific Integrated Circuit)

A custom integrated circuit designed for a specific application.

Examples:
- AI accelerators
- Network processors
- Mobile chips

---

## ASML

A major semiconductor equipment company producing advanced lithography systems, including EUV scanners.

Importance:
Enables leading-edge semiconductor manufacturing.

---

## ASIC Flow

The complete process from RTL design to manufactured chip:

RTL → Synthesis → Physical Design → Signoff → Tapeout

---

## Aspect Ratio

The ratio between chip height and width in physical design.

Important for:
- Floorplanning
- Routing efficiency
