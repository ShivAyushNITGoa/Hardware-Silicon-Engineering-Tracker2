# E Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## EDA — Electronic Design Automation
Software tools used for semiconductor design, simulation, verification, and manufacturing.

Examples:
- Cadence
- Synopsys
- Siemens EDA

## EEPROM — Electrically Erasable Programmable Read Only Memory
Non-volatile memory technology.

## Electromigration
Movement of metal atoms caused by high current density.

Impact:
- Reliability failure
- Interconnect damage

## EUV — Extreme Ultraviolet Lithography
Advanced lithography technology using 13.5 nm wavelength light.

Applications:
- 7nm
- 5nm
- 3nm manufacturing

## Etching
Process of selectively removing material from wafer surfaces.

Types:
- Wet etching
- Dry etching
- Reactive ion etching

## Embedded System
Computer system integrated into a device for dedicated functions.

Examples:
- Automotive controllers
- IoT devices
- Industrial systems
