# P-Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## PDK — Process Design Kit
Collection of foundry files required for IC design.

Includes:
- Design rules
- Device models
- Libraries
- Verification decks

## Photolithography
Process used to transfer patterns onto semiconductor wafers.

Steps:
- Resist coating
- Exposure
- Development
- Pattern transfer

## PMOS
P-channel MOS transistor.

Characteristics:
- Uses holes as carriers
- Used with NMOS in CMOS

## Placement
ASIC physical design stage where cells are positioned on the chip.

Goals:
- Timing optimization
- Congestion reduction

## PLL — Phase Locked Loop
Circuit used for clock generation and synchronization.

Applications:
- CPUs
- Communication systems
- SoCs
