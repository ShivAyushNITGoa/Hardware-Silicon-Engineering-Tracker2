# Y Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## Yield
Percentage of functional chips obtained from a manufactured wafer.

Factors:
- Defects
- Process variation
- Contamination

## Yield Engineering
Methods used to improve semiconductor manufacturing efficiency.

Techniques:
- Defect analysis
- Statistical process control
- Failure analysis

## Y-Chart
Design representation separating:
- Behavioral view
- Structural view
- Physical view
