# W Terms — Semiconductor Glossary

## Wafer
A thin slice of semiconductor crystal used as the base for IC fabrication.

Applications:
- CMOS manufacturing
- Memory chips
- Power devices

## Wafer Fabrication
Complete process of creating integrated circuits on silicon wafers.

Includes:
- Lithography
- Etching
- Deposition
- Implantation

## Wire Bonding
Packaging technique connecting die pads to package leads using thin wires.

## WAT — Wafer Acceptance Test
Electrical testing performed on wafers before packaging.

## Well Formation
CMOS fabrication step creating N-well and P-well regions.
