# Semiconductor Materials Dictionary

## Silicon (Si)
Most widely used semiconductor material.

Properties:
- Mature manufacturing
- Good oxide quality
- Low cost

## Gallium Nitride (GaN)
Wide bandgap semiconductor.

Applications:
- Power electronics
- RF devices
- Fast chargers

## Silicon Carbide (SiC)
Wide bandgap material used for:
- EV power electronics
- High voltage devices

## Germanium (Ge)
Early semiconductor material.

Uses:
- High mobility devices
- Research applications

## Compound Semiconductor
Materials made from multiple elements.

Examples:
- GaAs
- GaN
- SiC

## Wide Bandgap Semiconductor
Materials with large energy gaps enabling:
- High temperature operation
- High voltage handling
- High frequency performance
